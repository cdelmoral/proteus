# Activate the T3 service during explicit enrollment

T3 Enrollment authorizes and links Proteus and establishes a persistent background service, while Provisioning installs only the CLI and Provider Credential Enrollment remains separate. Completion verifies saved Connect authorization and environment linking plus a systemd service that is active, enabled for boot, and persistent after logout; remote access is verified by connecting from another device. We removed link-file timestamp comparisons, startup polling, and pinned-runtime validation because these internal checks could reject a working service; service upgrades and disruptive repairs remain explicit operations, and enrollment preserves completed steps on failure.
